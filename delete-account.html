Delete your account

You can request deletion of your The Super Bowl account and its associated data at any time. Here's how.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open The Super Bowl app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Account settings → Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and personal data will be scheduled for deletion.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll process your request.

Send to trust.dev303@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account", and include the name and phone number on your account.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Account profile (name, phone, email)Permanently deleted
Saved addresses & preferencesPermanently deleted
Order historyDeleted, except records we must keep for tax/legal reasons
Payment detailsRemoved from your account (card data is held by our payment provider)

Timing & retention

Requests are processed within 30 days. Some information may be retained for a limited period where required by law (for example, transaction records for accounting), after which it is deleted.
